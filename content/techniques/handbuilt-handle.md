**Where did the handles come from?** For 1,953 pieces the reviewed catalog showed none. Then [[range-1951-2000|the 1951–2000 run]] grew twelve: six mugs with a single rough loop ([[1954]], [[1971]], [[1986]], [[1994]], [[1996]], [[1998]]), two cups with an ear on each side ([[1992]], [[1993]]), and four two-handled amphora-form vessels ([[1995]], [[1997]], [[1999]], [[2000]]). Each loop is built and attached by hand, with pinch marks and join seams left showing, some pierced wide, some twisted like rope.

**How are they made?** By hand, on the maker's own word: every handle in the catalog is built and joined by hand. What the photographs show is the join, thumbed seams and torn ends, and on the white pair [[2068]] and [[2069]] a sideways twist left exactly where his hand stopped.

The term records that a handle is *there*, as photographed. Strength, comfort, and whether a finger fits are things a photo can't testify to, so no page claims them.
