**What are the raised lines?** Directional striations dragged through the surface while it could still move, leaving raised parallel tracks rather than cut-in scratches. On [[2374]] the tracks sweep in circular passes across the whole top face; on [[2377]] they run in dense straighter lines. The raised profile is what separates this from [[scored-marks|scoring]], which cuts into the clay instead.

Earlier cases live in the cup catalog: brushed passes on 1803 and 1808, and a belly sweep on [[2287]], one of the pierced boulders.
