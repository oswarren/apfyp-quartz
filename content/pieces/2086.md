![Top-down view into a handmade ceramic cup, pale glossy interior with a faint spiral at the floor and black glaze tracking one edge of the rim, piece 2086 of A Penny For Your Pottery.](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0628/8434/8065/files/PXL-20260805_100626720.jpg?v=1786024174)

A round-bellied handmade cup, egg-shaped and standing on a small foot, its cream-white ground carrying gestural [[brushed-glaze|brushed decoration]]. On the front, a thick black line runs the width of the body, thinning and swelling as it goes, and swings into a large soft black mass at one edge; teal and turquoise washes sit beneath and around it like watercolour, and a blue drip runs down from the rim.

Come round the back and the black takes over. A single deep field covers most of that wall, cream breaking through it in bays and inlets — and the field is pocked with round craters that open onto pale clay underneath, a dozen or so scattered across it, each with a raised lip. It is the loudest surface on the cup and the quietest colour.

The interior photographs pale cream and glossy, a faint spiral turning in the floor. The foot is bare clay, ringed with spiral marks and carrying no written number. Its six photos were taken on August 5, 2026 — [see everything photographed that day](../tags/batch/2026-08-05).

**Is the pattern printed or painted?** Painted — the photos show stroke direction, thin spots, and edges where colours overlap; no two of these cups repeat a mark. Piece 2,086 of 10,000, listed at $20.86.
