![Large closed dark boulder form held in a hand, its coarse surface opened in places to paler clay beneath, piece 2289 of A Penny For Your Pottery.](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0628/8434/8065/files/PXL-20260716_140050629.jpg?v=1785755532)

The biggest of the July 16 boulders — it fills a spread hand in the opening photo, where the others sit comfortably inside one. A closed [[handbuilding|handbuilt]] form in coarse dark [[bare-clay|bare clay]], and the only one in the group opened up: controlled thermal shock has broken the dark outer surface in places to reveal the paler clay inside, showing as cream lines and short curling arcs around the flank and a longer break down one side. It is also the most heavily grained of the eight, with [[embedded-particles|pale particles]] standing across the whole body, from fine pepper on the crown to coarse grains along the belly. The [[pierced-hole|hole]] sits in a shallow crater at the top, its rim torn and folded, with a dried grass stem set into it in the second photo.

**What are the pale lines on 2289?** Not a coating — they are the inside of the clay itself, opened up by controlled thermal shock. Piece 2289 of 10,000, listed at $22.89.
