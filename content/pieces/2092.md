A small jar with a round, bulbous body and a short neck opening outward to a raised collar. Most of the surface is smooth, glossy cream, but the shoulder wears a broad band of rough, granular, sand-like texture in tan and rust, and cream drips cross down into that band as if the smooth coat reached over its edge. Below, the body settles onto a small foot ring. The single base photo shows an incised marking at the center, its characters too faint to read, so this page claims no number.
