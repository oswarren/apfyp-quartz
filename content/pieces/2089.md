A straight-sided handmade cup, slightly waisted, rim running uneven, bare pale clay showing at the foot. The ground is cloud: pale cream washed through with soft blue and blue-grey, warm tan surfacing in patches, a darker blue-grey line drawn round the rim — a [[brushed-glaze|brushed ground]] with no hard edges anywhere in it.

Over that, in black, one word written again and again: **skating**. It runs in three staggered rows around the wall — "skating", "skating", "Skating" — some upright, some sloping, the letters thinning where the brush ran dry and pooling where it didn't, each pass a slightly different size from the one above it. These are [[glaze-text|handwritten words]] in the plainest sense: the same word, over and over, until it wrapped the cup.

Looking in, the interior is pale cream-grey and glossy with a faint spiral in the floor, and the rim speckles blue where the outside washes came over the edge.
