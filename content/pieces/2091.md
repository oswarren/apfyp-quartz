A small waisted [[handbuilding|handmade]] cup — wide through the belly, drawing in to a short straight neck, sitting on a narrow foot. The ground is pale cream, streaked horizontally with soft blue and mottled grey-green, glossy over it all, the kind of [[brushed-glaze|brushed ground]] that looks like weather seen through a window.

The writing sits right on top of it in black. "I" stands alone up near the rim with a blue dot beside it; "kee" runs across the middle; and along the bottom, clear as a caption, **it's nothing**. Carry on around the cup and another line starts — "I wa" — before a solid black field takes the wall and the sentence goes behind it. They are [[glaze-text|handwritten words]], printed rather than joined, sized by whatever room was left.

Underneath, on the bare base over its spiral rings, [[numbered-bases|"2091" is handwritten]] in black.

**Which cup is this in the store?** The listing carries no descriptive title — but the cup titles itself twice over: "it's nothing" on the wall, "2091" on the base. Piece 2,091 of 10,000, listed at $20.91.
