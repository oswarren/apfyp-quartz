![Overhead view into a handmade ceramic cup, bare clay interior with fine concentric rings and a mottled dark band at the rim, piece 2082 of A Penny For Your Pottery.](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0628/8434/8065/files/PXL-20260805_095317269.jpg?v=1786023883)

A tall straight-sided cup, waisted a little at the middle, its rim running uneven the whole way round. The wall is weather rather than pattern: mottled blue-green and teal washing over cream, warm patches of bare clay surfacing through, a darker blue-grey field banking up one side and a dark line drawn along the rim. Rust-amber pin-spots sit in it here and there.

Across the middle of that wall, in black, are [[glaze-text|handwritten words]]: "I've" above, "writ" below, brushed in a plain hand that slopes gently downhill as the wall curves away, then carries on around the cup. Everything else here is [[brushed-glaze|brushed decoration]].

The two overhead views go the other way entirely: the interior is bare grey-brown clay, unglazed, turning in spiral forming rings, with one fine line tracking across the floor of the well and a pale glazed lip above it. Underneath, on the bare base, [[numbered-bases|"2082" is handwritten]] in black.

**Is that really handwriting?** Plain letters, brushed straight onto the wall — "I've" over "writ" — sloping downhill the way a hand does when the surface curves out from under it. Piece 2082 of 10,000, listed at $20.82.
