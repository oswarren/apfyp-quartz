A cream cylindrical tumbler with a slightly uneven rim, its lower body wrapped in an asymmetric blue-black wash that pools and runs. The wash sits differently on each side: heavy with narrow near-black horizontal streaks on one, broken into darker patches and short marks on another, thinned to pale teal streaks above a solid navy band on a third. Small near-black [[speckled-surfaces|specks]] scatter through the blue wherever it lands. The interior is a solid glossy gray-tan. The base is largely [[bare-clay|unglazed buff clay]] with concentric ring impressions at its center, [[numbered-bases|"2099" hand-inscribed]] in black, and a partial black arc reaching the foot edge.

**Does the blue wrap all the way around?** Every side shows some of it, always riding the lower half and never in the same arrangement twice. Piece 2099 of 10,000, listed at $20.99.
