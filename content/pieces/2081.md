A small round cup, no handle, standing on a narrow foot — and one face does all the talking. Turn the cup and the storm clears. The far face is mostly quiet cream — one turquoise patch at its edge, a scatter of small dark blobs, a few rust-amber pin-spots. Looking straight down, the interior runs cream-grey and glossy, peppered with dark specks over a soft spiral in the well. The last view tips the cup onto its side: a bare pale foot ring, and one broad black stroke crossing the wall just above it.
