A range of one, for now: the terracotta bowl [[2292]] follows straight after the boulder run of [[range-2280-2289|pieces 2280–2289]]. Its wall is deep terracotta-red [[bare-clay|bare clay]], finely pitted and set with small pale [[embedded-particles|embedded flecks]]; its rim runs uneven and undulating, ringed by a dark glaze line where the red exterior meets a glossy ivory-cream glazed interior; and its body tapers to a rounded base with no foot ring, the number [[numbered-bases|handwritten]] there in a pale hand.

One photographed piece makes the shortest range yet. The two numbers before it, 2290 and 2291, remain reserved and unphotographed, so 2292 is the only piece here with a page.
