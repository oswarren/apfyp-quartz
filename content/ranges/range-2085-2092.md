Seven photographed pieces: six white handmade cups split between two looks, quiet speckled cream and loud abstract brushwork, plus one small jar at the span's end. Every piece's price is its number times one cent, so the run goes from \$20.85 to \$20.92. One number inside the span, 2087, has no photographed listing and no page.

The six cups are handmade [[range-2250-2260|cups like the documented 2250–2260 run]], photographing cream-white. [[2085]] and [[2088]] were shot on [April 21, 2026](../tags/batch/2026-04-21), and most of the painted cups on [August 5, 2026](../tags/batch/2026-08-05), many ending on a photograph of their numbered base. [[2092]]'s listing was re-photographed on [August 13, 2026](../tags/batch/2026-08-13) and now shows a small sand-collared jar; its page describes the current photos.

- **The painted four** — gestural [[brushed-glaze|brushed decoration]] in black, blue, and frosty white: [[2086]], [[2089]], [[2090]], [[2091]]
- **The ones that talk** — [[glaze-text|handwritten words]] in the glaze: [[2089]] ("skating", over and over), [[2091]] ("it's nothing")
- **The jar** — [[2092]], glossy cream with a rough sand-textured shoulder band
- **Numbers on the base** — [[2085]], [[2089]], [[2090]] and [[2091]] carry their numbers [[numbered-bases|handwritten]] on the bare foot
