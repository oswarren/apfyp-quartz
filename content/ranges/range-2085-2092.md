Seven photographed cups: white, handmade, and split between two looks — quiet speckled cream and loud abstract brushwork. Every piece's price is its number times one cent, so the run goes from \$20.85 to \$20.92. One number inside the span, 2087, has no photographed listing and no page.

All seven are [[handbuilding|handbuilt]] [[range-2250-2260|cups like the documented 2250–2260 run]], photographing cream-white. They come from two photography days: [[2085]] and [[2088]] were shot on [April 21, 2026](../tags/batch/2026-04-21), and the rest were shot on [August 5, 2026](../tags/batch/2026-08-05), most of them ending on a photograph of their numbered base.

- **The quiet pair** — fine dark [[speckled-surfaces|speckling]] over warm white: [[2085]], [[2088]]
- **The painted five** — gestural [[brushed-glaze|brushed decoration]] in black, blue, and frosty white: [[2086]], [[2089]], [[2090]], [[2091]], [[2092]]
- **The ones that talk** — [[glaze-text|handwritten words]] in the glaze: [[2089]] ("skating", over and over), [[2091]] ("it's nothing"), [[2092]]
- **Numbers on the base** — [[2085]], [[2089]], [[2090]] and [[2091]] carry their numbers [[numbered-bases|handwritten]] on the bare foot
