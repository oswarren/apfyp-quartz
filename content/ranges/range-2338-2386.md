Forty-eight flat slabs, photographed together on the morning of August 16, 2026: the catalog's first run that isn't a vessel at all. No rims, no feet, no interiors. Each is a roughly square sheet of clay with irregular edges, a decorated top face, and a dark underside carrying its [[numbered-bases|handwritten number]].

The sameness of the format pushes all the variety into the surfaces, and the variety is wide. The run opens pale: 2338 through 2342 wear quiet grey top faces with a dark band at the edge, and [[2338]] alone carries trailed cream markings. From 2343 the tops turn striated and the undersides pick up [[scored-marks|scored cross-hatch]]. The late 2340s go glossy black with matte tan patches breaking through, the 2350s run streaked and banded (with [[2361]] a pale outlier in a dark stretch), and the 2360s settle into mottled browns. Then the run gets bolder: [[2371]] and [[2372]] are a matched pair swirled in cream and dark marbling around a dark center, [[2374]] and [[2377]] carry [[dragged-comb|comb-dragged striations]], 2376 through 2378 each hold a single glossy patch against a matte ground, the rounded-corner squares of 2379 through 2383 follow, and [[2385]] closes the run with a pale spiral wound around a sharp dark rectangle.

Three pieces carry visible condition marks, noted plainly on their pages: a crack along one edge of [[2345]], a thin fissure line on [[2353]]'s face, and a hairline crack on the back of [[2385]].

The run has one gap: 2369 is absent from the store export that produced these pages, so it has no page here.
