<section class="as-hero">
<h1>For people with more ideas than places to put them.</h1>
<p class="as-lede">Send the notes, photographs, drafts, experiments, and half-finished projects as they are. I turn them into a connected private archive and a public website that makes the larger body of work visible.</p>
<p class="as-cta-row"><a class="as-cta" href="https://apennyforyourpottery.com/pages/contact">Start with a sample</a></p>
<p class="as-quiet">You shouldn't have to stop making in order to organize what you've made.</p>
</section>

<section class="as-transform">
<h2>One real transformation</h2>
<p class="as-intro">Watch one piece move from scattered files to a page anyone can explore. Yours might be notes, research, or half-finished projects instead of photographs — the method doesn't change: gather the fragments, make the relationships visible, and build from there.</p>
<p class="as-proof">This entire archive began as scattered photographs, partial records, old store listings, and half-finished notes. I use the same system for my own work — the site you're reading now was built from it.</p>

<h2>What you receive</h2>
<div class="as-outcomes">
<div class="as-outcome"><h3>A private archive</h3><p>One connected place for photographs, notes, records, ideas, research, and unfinished material. Only you see it.</p></div>
<div class="as-outcome"><h3>A public website</h3><p>A browsable site built from the parts you choose and approve. Nothing uncertain is published as fact.</p></div>
<div class="as-outcome"><h3>A system that keeps growing</h3><p>New work drops into the same structure as you make it. No rebuilding each time.</p></div>
</div>
